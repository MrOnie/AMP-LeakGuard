\section*{Formal Declaration}

The author declares that they have \textbf{no known competing financial interests or personal relationships} that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

Specifically, the author certifies that:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Employment and Affiliations:} This study was conducted exclusively as part of academic research at the Escuela Politécnica Nacional (Quito, Ecuador). The author does not hold employment, consultancy, or advisory board positions with any pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or commercial software entity.
    \item \textbf{Financial Support and Grants:} The author has not received any direct or indirect financial compensation, honoraria, stock ownership, or proprietary funding from commercial organizations related to the development of the \texttt{AMP-LeakGuard} software or the benchmarking of antimicrobial peptides.

\end{enumerate}
